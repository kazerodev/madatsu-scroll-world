# 02 - Concept and visual direction

## Subject

**MADATSU TCG - "Drawer to Display"**

MADATSU is my own trading card shop in Mechelen (madatsutcg.com). We buy cards from players and collectors. This site is the landing page for that service: "sell us your cards".

I chose a real use case on purpose. After the assignment I can link this page from Instagram, Discord and the webshop.

## Audience

- Players and collectors in Belgium (mostly Flanders/Brussels), 16-35.
- People who have a box of cards they don't use anymore: old Pokémon, One Piece, trade leftovers.
- They don't know what their cards are worth, and they don't trust shops that give low offers.

## Core message

"Your old cards are worth something. We check them fairly, give a clear offer, and put them back into play."

## The journey (4 scenes + 3 transitions)

The whole film follows ONE card. That card is the thread that makes the four places feel like one world.

| # | Scene | What we see | Copy idea |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | The drawer | An open shoebox of loose cards in a drawer, desk lamp, evening. One card lies face-up with a holo shine. | "Cards you never play?" |
| T1 | Drawer -> desk | Camera glides forward over the box and down to the holo card. The card slides into a clear toploader. Wood becomes navy felt. | |
| 2 | The check | The card in a toploader on a navy felt playmat, a loupe and a warm lamp. Other cards lined up in sleeves. | "We check every card by hand." |
| T2 | Desk -> counter | Low camera tracks forward along the mat, the shop opens up behind it. | |
| 3 | The offer | The card on the shop counter, sealed boxes and binders on shelves in soft focus. | "A clear offer. You decide." |
| T3 | Counter -> display | Camera continues forward past the counter to a lit glass display case. | |
| 4 | The display | The card on a small acrylic stand inside a glass case with LED light, between other cards. | "Ready for its next collector." + CTA |

## Camera style

- Architecture: always forward. Slow dolly, low height, product-macro feel (think 35-50 mm lens, shallow depth of field).
- Inside a scene: a slow push-in with a little parallax.
- In a transition: continue forward, following the card. Never pull back.
- Each clip starts and ends with a calm, steady forward drift (motion handoff).

## Visual direction

**Mood:** quiet, warm, careful. Like a collector handling something valuable at night. Not flashy gaming RGB.

**Lighting:** one warm practical light per scene (desk lamp, shop pendant, LED in the case) against dark navy surroundings. Holo cards catch the light.

**Materials:** worn wood, cardboard, navy felt playmat, clear PVC toploaders, acrylic, glass. Matte surfaces vs shiny plastic and holo foil.

**Palette** (from the MADATSU logo: white wordmark on navy):

| Name | Hex | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Navy (brand) | `#13213F` | Felt, shop walls, page background |
| Night | `#0A1020` | Deep shadows, page base |
| Lamp | `#F0A94B` | Warm light, accent, CTA |
| Paper | `#F3EEE4` | Text, cardboard highlights |
| Holo | `#8FD6CF` | Small accents, foil reflections |

**Typography:**

- Headlines: Archivo (wide, bold). Strong and simple, fits a shop brand.
- Labels and numbers: IBM Plex Mono. References the label on a graded card slab (set number, grade, ID).
- Body: Archivo regular.

**Interface motif:** each text panel looks like the label of a graded card slab: a thin frame, a top strip in mono (`MADATSU - 01/04 - THE DRAWER`) and a big headline under it. The progress rail on the side counts like a set number: `001/004`.

## Rules for the generated images

- No readable text, no logos, no real card art inside the video. Card faces are always abstract or blurred, or shown from the back. (Models deform text and art, and I want the video to stay clean.)
- Real card photos (I have permission from the distributor) are used in the interface, not in the video.
- No people. Only hands would be possible, but they deform easily, so I leave them out.
- Same lens, height and color grade in every scene.

## Ending / CTA

The last scene holds on the display case. The CTA panel says "Get an offer" and opens WhatsApp with a prepared message. Under the film there is a short normal section: how it works, what we pay, a small form that builds the WhatsApp message, and contact info.
